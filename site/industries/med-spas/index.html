The leaks

Where med spas lose money every single week

  • Leak 1The 9pm Instagram DM

    Aesthetic clients research in the evening and at weekends, when your front desk has gone home. A reply the next morning often comes too late: they've already booked with the clinic that answered first.

    The fixAn AI agent trained on your menu replies on Instagram, Facebook, website chat and text within seconds, answers treatment and price questions, and books the consultation.

  • Leak 2The call you miss mid-treatment

    Your injector is with a client and the phone rings out. Most callers don't leave a voicemail. They call the next med spa on Google.

    The fixMissed call text back sends a text within seconds and keeps the conversation going until they're booked.

  • Leak 3The free consult that never shows

    Consultations booked on impulse get forgotten, and a provider's hour disappears.

    The fixA deposit or card on file at booking, reminders by text and email, and an automatic rebooking message if they miss it.

  • Leak 4The Botox client who drifts past month four

    Neurotoxin results typically fade within 3 to 6 months. Without a reminder, clients wait too long, or book wherever is running a promotion that week.

    The fixA rebooking reminder timed to each client's last treatment, with a one-tap link to book.

  • Leak 5The laser package that stops at session three

    Laser hair removal usually takes several sessions spaced weeks apart. Every skipped session is lost revenue and a weaker result, which becomes a weaker review.

    The fixThe next session is booked and reminded after every visit, and missed sessions get chased automatically.

  • Leak 6The happy client who never joins a membership

    One-off clients are expensive to replace. Members come back every month and spend more.

    The fixA membership offer after the right visit, with recurring billing that runs itself.

  • Leak 7Five-star results, no Google review

    Clients love their results and still don't leave a review, because nobody asked at the right moment.

    The fixA review request a few days after the treatment, when results show and clients are happiest.

  • Leak 8Hundreds of past clients sitting in your booking software

    Your cheapest new bookings are clients you've already treated. Most clinics never contact them again.

    The fixA reactivation campaign to your past client list that books them straight back into your calendar.
